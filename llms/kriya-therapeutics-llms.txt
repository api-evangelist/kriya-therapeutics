# Kriya Therapeutics

> Kriya Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company founded in 2019 that
> designs, develops and manufactures one-time AAV gene therapies for chronic diseases affecting
> large patient populations. It is headquartered in Research Triangle Park (Durham, North Carolina)
> with operations in Palo Alto, California, and has raised over $900 million. Its pipeline spans
> ophthalmology (geographic atrophy, thyroid eye disease), metabolic disease (Type 1 diabetes,
> MASH/NASH) and neurology (trigeminal neuralgia), supported by in-house GMP manufacturing from 1L
> research scale to 3,000L bioreactor scale.
