Ryan Gallagher is a Senior Research Lead in the Patient Square Insights group at Patient Square Capital. Most recently, he was a Principal in the health care practice at Boston Consulting Group where he specialized in biotechnology and pharmaceuticals. His doctoral research focused on development and application of high-throughput genome engineering technologies.
